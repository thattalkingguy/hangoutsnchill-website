# A Letter to the First Person Who Joins HangoutsNChill

Welcome.

If you're reading this, it means you've become part of something that began long before you arrived.

HangoutsNChill wasn't created because I wanted to build another app.

It was created because I believe people deserve opportunities.

I have seen talented people struggle, not because they lacked ability, but because they lacked access, guidance, and the right platform.

This company exists to change that.

Here, we don't measure success only by revenue.

We measure success by the number of lives we help transform.

Every creator who earns their first income...

Every student who gains confidence...

Every entrepreneur who turns an idea into a business...

Those are our victories.

I don't expect perfection.

I expect honesty.

I expect humility.

I expect curiosity.

I expect the courage to admit mistakes, learn from them, and keep improving.

We treat our users with respect because they are trusting us with more than money.

They are trusting us with their dreams.

Never forget that.

There will be days when the servers fail.

There will be bugs.

There will be difficult customers.

There will be moments when everything seems impossible.

On those days, remember why this company exists.

Remember the people waiting for opportunities.

Remember the creators depending on us.

Remember that every problem we solve removes a barrier for someone else.

That is why we do this.

Around here, we celebrate progress.

We don't wait for perfection before we move.

You'll probably hear one phrase more than any other.

"Let's go."

It isn't just something we say.

It's who we are.

It means we keep moving.

We keep learning.

We keep building.

No matter how difficult yesterday was.

One more thing...

If you ever have to choose between what is profitable and what is right...

Choose what is right.

Profit follows trust.

Trust never follows greed.

Thank you for believing in this vision.

Let's build something that outlives all of us.

Welcome to HangoutsNChill.

— Ola Olabode

Founder
